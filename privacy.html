Before You Land

Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026. Applies to the Before You Land app for iPhone and Android.

In short: Before You Land collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your home country, favourite and recent countries, packing lists and templates, temperature unit, and your passport's expiry and issue dates (if you enter them), in the app's own storage. The app also saves the last exchange rates and weather readings so it can work offline. None of this is sent anywhere. Delete the app to remove it all.

When the app goes online

  • Exchange rates: at most once a day, the app asks ExchangeRate-API for the day's rates. The request contains nothing about you.
  • Current weather: the app sends the coordinates of the city you're viewing (a capital or major city bundled with the app, never your own location) to MET Norway. A reading is reused for up to an hour.
  • Official advice links: tapping "Official entry requirements" opens a government or IATA page in your browser.

As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. They have their own privacy policies. The app doesn't use your location and doesn't ask for location permission.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer receives no data from the app. We don't sell or share anything, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
